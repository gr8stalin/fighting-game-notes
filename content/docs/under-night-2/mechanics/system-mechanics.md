Like other anime fighters, UNI 2 has several mechanics that influence interactions.

## The GRD Gauge and Vorpal State

Both players share a singular meter located at the bottom-center of the screen called the GRD Gauge. When a round starts, both players have 0 GRD.

Both players accrue GRD over the course of a round through gameplay. Every 17 seconds, the game will check which player has more GRD, and the player with more GRD will receive a passive buff called **Vorpal State**.

Most of the time there will be a clear winner for the passive buff, but the game does recognize when both players have an equivalent amount of GRD during the meter check, and will award a less potent Vorpal State to both players.

### Earning and losing GRD

Both players earn GRD passively merely by being in the round. Each player is limited to passively earning 5 GRD blocks, and the rate of accrual rises the closer both players are to one another while at midscreen.

However, if one player is on the defensive in the corner, their passive GRD accrual will be slower while the aggressing player's GRD accrual will be faster.

Both players can actively earn GRD by interacting with their opponent.

#### Neutral

Moving around midscreen and using your movement to influence the opponent actively earns GRD:

- Walking forward
- Running/dashing forward
- Opponent cedes space to your forward movement by:
  - Backwalking
  - Backjumping
  - Backdashing

Not moving or ceding space to the other player sacrifices GRD:

- Backwalking
- Backjumping
- Backdashing
  - Regular backdashes sacrifice 1 GRD
  - Reversal timing backdashes (i.e. backdashing after being knocked down to escape a throw) sacrifice 0.5 GRD
- Opponent takes the space you cede

#### Vorpal State assignment

When the game checks the state of the GRD meter and assigns Vorpal State, the player entering Vorpal State earns 1 GRD.

#### EXS Actions

Pressing and holding [D] to trigger Concentrate state awards GRD meter/second.
