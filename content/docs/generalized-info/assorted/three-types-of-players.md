During Sub Sunday, Zaf recommended the Core A Gaming video about "The Three Types of Fighting Gamers" as a way to categorize people on ranked.

The video presents three different archetypes: Brains, Heart, and Body

Brains like working with frame data and options selects.

Heart players like making reads and playing in the heat of the moment. They're usually the type of players who latch onto grapplers, or specifically to SF6, will drive rush a lot to set up

Body players like execution and the reward from it, usually high damage and mixups. They're the type of player to wait for you to make a move so they can react with some option.
