Mago goes over why you drill things like antiairs so they become second nature; if there's a hard to deal with mechanic like Drive Impact, you can dedicate most of your focus to looking for the signs. If you detect that the opponent has left the ground, your muscle memory kicks in.

Grinding out execution for antiairs and bnbs means when your opponent is doing crazy shit, you can dedicate your mental stack to dealing with the crazy shit. If they jump, you've ingrained anti-air into your psyche to where you do it without thinking.

It's not that you can't react, it's that you have to think about how you're going to react instead of instinctively doing it.
