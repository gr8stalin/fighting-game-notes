I got into fighting games as a "real" adult, just before my 30s. I spent the years prior taking RTS games pretty seriously as a core and somewhat competitive player and taking FPS games seriously as a competitor.

I didn't have a "child's exploration" part of my life with fighting games (vast amount of free time + child's neuroplasticity), so I've had to rely on external resources to put things together.

These are videos that have helped in some manner to piece together how to play fighting games at a reasonable level.
