This is a somewhat SF5 specific video but it goes over one of the most important things that people don't put enough value into: training mode and training with others.

Brian talks about using training mode to learn what punishes are for various things at varying ranges, and then stresses that the only way to get these things to be muscle memory is to focus on them in matches.

People playing in lobbies will give you a lower-stress but real-world environment for committing those punishes.
