Only watch the first half of the video (ends at 7:35). Applicable to most games that have autoplayback functions in their training modes.

If you have a bad habit of responding with a poor option to some action under pressure (i.e., you press a bad button for antiairing that sometimes gets the job done but otherwise fucks you up), a great way to break that habit is to set up a dummy to do three-ish variations of the situation you respond poorly to and then two-ish options that are completely different approaches.

For example, to respond with the best antiair for a situation, record three different jumpin attempts and then two ground options to keep you in check. Your goal is to respond to the ground options to simply stop them, and then respond to the jumpin options with the antiair you're supposed to use.

If your character has different follow up options based on the timing of the anti-air, incorporate them into the training. Brian said that he's spent two hours training this kind of thing with Oro's antiair shenanigans, just doing the varying follow-ups based on when Oro's stand mp hits or if it trades.
