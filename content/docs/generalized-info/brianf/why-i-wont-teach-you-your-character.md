Brian talks about how people going "hey [x] how do I play [character]" with that specificity is not the move to getting better.

He made several videos about using Street Fighter 5's training mode and various options, and his fanbase asked him for those videos but for their specific characters. 

You need to do the work, not [pro player making content]. They'll help if they can, but you have to be the one to do the ground work.
