Robert Garcia picks up the tengu mask and becomes the modern Mr. Karate for South Town.

## What is he good at?

Mr. Karate kinda has everything: fireball, dragon punch reversal, good normals, good damage per interaction, a *counter???* He's a genuinely versatile character that has a lot going for him, including a layer of mix on his heavy DP where he can charge it on offense as a delay jab trap and *still* brake it on hit.

## Where is he weak?

His walk speed isn't the best? There *is* a bit of an adjustment period because he's not exactly a traditional shoto; his fireball has slow startup and a charge gimmick, and he doesn't really have any safe specials (even though 236D on block is a knowledge check early on), so you have to play a bit more carefully.

Mr. Karate doesn't really have a lot of glaring flaws to the point where EVO 2026 and EWC 2026 had a fair amount of pros picking up Mr. Karate to try to have an easier time getting the bag, but their reliance on Mr. Karate's counter and swapping from their more familiar mains was occasionally their undoing.
