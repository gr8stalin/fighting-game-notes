The booba ninja. Sort of an analogue to Chun-Li from Street Fighter, her varied movement options and good normals allow her to control the neutral with decent offensive and defensive options.

## What is she good at?

Mai is good at footsies/neutral. Between having great movespeed, air options, and a lot of normals that are built for zoning, poking, and whiff punishing, she's great at controlling the ground and air.

## Where is she weak?

She has zero atraditional mix: the most you're getting out of Mai's offense is strike-throw-shimmy. Her damage per interaction isn't the best unless you're doing some of her more technical combos that require tiger knee super B.
