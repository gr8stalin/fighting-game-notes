<br/>

Mai's combo structure is relatively simple. She has some "saucy" stuff hidden in corner combos and tiger knee supers, but she trades combo depth for good midscreen control.

## Terminology

\* = Break

(#X >) = Optional starting move

## Day 1 Combos

### Light starters

|Combo|Notes|
|-----|-----|
|(2B >) 2A/5A xx 236B/214C|The "clipped 'em in da neuch" combo. 236B gives more carry, 214C gives more damage.|
|(2B >) 5A xx 623D|Your light confirm into stuff combo. This is an important starter that you'll build on.|
|cl.B > 2C xx 623D* > 214AC xx 214BD > 236D* > 214C|Close light meaty combo, i.e., your light that links to a heavy on any hit. Can replace 214C with any super.|

<br/>

The cl.B > 2C link for the third combo is a little finnicky because it's a link (surprise!) but cl.B is +3 on block, so you can send 2C on offense and try to hitconfirm from there. cl.B does have some odd pushback so be careful with your spacing when just sending the 2C after it.

### Heavy Starters and Jumpins

|Combo|Notes|
|-----|-----|
|5C/2C xx 214AC xx 214BD > 236D* > 623D* > 236B|Poke conversion. 236B gives more damage and corner carry. Can replace 236B with any super.|
|5D > feint > cl.C xx 214AC xx 214BD > 236D* > 623D* > 236B|Close anti-guard-cancel meaty combo.|
|jD > cl.C xx 214AC xx 214BD > 236D* > 623D* > 236B|Jumpin/Hopin. Can replace 236B with any super.|

### Punishes

|Starter + Combo|Notes|
|---------------|-----|
|214D > 5C xx 623D* > 236D* > 623B|Optimal-ish meterless DP punish|
