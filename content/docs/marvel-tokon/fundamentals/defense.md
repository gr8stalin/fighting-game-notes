<br/>

Defense in Tokon leverages familiar mechanics from various older fighting games. Traditional blocking is still available, and there's no penalty for blocking like in Street Fighter 6.

4L + A performs "Assemble Counter". This is similar to the V-Shift function in Street Fighter 5: you can press 4L + A in reaction to the startup of an attack, dodge it, and unleash a small counterattack combo with a teammate.

4L + A changes a little on block. The technique becomes known as "Crossover" and a teammate will strike your opponent to push them away from you. It does a small amount of damage but serves as a way to stop your opponent's offense.

You can 4L + A your opponent's 4L + A.

A special note for all team mechanics: if the teammate associated with the team mechanic is currently on screen, the mechanic may not function as usual, and in some cases may not function at all.
