Defense in Tokon leverages familiar mechanics from various older fighting games. Traditional blocking is still available, and there's no penalty for blocking like in Street Fighter 6.

## Assemble Counter

4L + A performs "Assemble Counter". This is similar to the V-Shift function in Street Fighter 5: you can press 4L + A in reaction to the startup of an attack, dodge it, and unleash a small counterattack combo with a teammate.

## Crossover

4L + A changes a little on block. The technique becomes known as "Crossover" and a teammate will strike your opponent to push them away from you. It does a small amount of damage but serves as a way to stop your opponent's offense.

You can 4L + A your opponent's 4L + A, causing a clash that returns to neutral.

Additionally, you can tag in the teammate who assisted your defense.

A special note: by default, crossover summons the teammate at 3 o'clock. If the teammate at 3 o'clock is currently on screen or recharging, holding down will summon the teammate at 6 o'clock, and holding left will summon the teammate at 9 o'clock.

### Perfect reflect

There's an additional layer to crossover. If you press crossover as your opponent's crossover is about to strike you, your teammate will perform a **perfect reflect** on your opponent's crossover.

This will temporarily shatter their Assembly Gauge and deal a slight amount of damage to your opponent, keeping advantage in your favor and denying your opponent access to *all* tag-related mechanics for a short time.

## Health

Tokon calls the HP gauge the **Vital Gauge**. This is an important distinction because that's how ArcSys chose to delineate regular health that can only decrease and grey health that can regenerate to a certain point (i.e. Street Fighter).

In Tokon, you start each round at 80000 health points - that's you and your starting teammate. Reuniting with your second teammate will increase your health to 90000, and completely reuniting with your team gives you 100000.

If you reunite with a teammate after taking damage, you'll see a grey translucent bar nested within your health bar. Tokon calls this the **Healing Gauge**, but as mentioned earlier, it's grey health.

You can still lose a round if you run out of health despite having grey health.
