## Button strengths

Tokon is a L/M/H-style game in the vein of Granblue Versus and Unist 2:

- L = Light
- M = Medium
- H = Heavy

## Autocombos

Autocombos are becoming very prevalent in modern fighters. By pressing L multiple times on hit, you'll do a small combo ending with all your active teammates striking your opponent.

## Attacking with your team

Pressing [A]ssist + Forward (6A) performs a projectile assist. The projectile will come from your character in the 3 o'clock position.

2A allows your teammate at 6 o'clock to strike your opponent, and pressing T afterward allows you to swap places with them.

L + A performs "Assemble Smash" which is the game's universal overhead. On hit, you'll deal some damage alongside one of your teammates automatically. If you have more than one bar in your Assembly Gauge, you can press A as your first teammate strikes your opponent to have your second teammate continue the combo.
