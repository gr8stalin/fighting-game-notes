## Day 1 Combos

Anything that starts with 5M can start with 2M and vice-versa, so you have similar routing for M poke and low.

There's not much shame in doing some of the autocombos this early in the game, similarly for link attacks. You can leverage the ability to mix & match enders to at least get oki and wallbreaks.

### Meterless BnBs

|Combo|Advantage|Damage|Notes|
|-----|---------|------|-----|
|5M > 2M xx 214M > 623M > jH > jc jH6H > j623L|+15|27020|Basic solo combo. Only wallbreaks when started from midscreen or in corner.|

### H Extension BnBs

|Combo|Advantage|Damage|Notes|
|-----|---------|------|-----|
|5M > 2M xx 214M > 623M > j6H > 623H > jH > jc jH xx j623L|+15 wallbreak|31410|Basic solo combo, carries from corner to corner with wallbreak.|

|2M > 5M > jc jM > jH > jc jH > jH > 5A > 5H xx 214M > 623M > jH > jc jH xx j623L|+15 wallbreak|30015|Cobbled together from a mix of Sajam's combo structure video and Meno's open beta BnB video.|
