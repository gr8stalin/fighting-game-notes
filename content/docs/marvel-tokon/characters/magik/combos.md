## Day 1 Combos

Anything that starts with 5M can start with 2M and vice-versa, so you have similar routing for M poke and low.

There's not much shame in doing some of the autocombos early in your time with the game, similarly for link attacks. You can leverage the ability to mix & match enders to at least get oki and wallbreaks.

### Meterless BnBs

|Combo|Advantage|Damage|Notes|
|-----|---------|------|-----|
|5M > 2M xx 214M > 623M > j6H > jc jH6H > j623L|+15|27020|Basic solo combo. Only wallbreaks when started from midscreen or in corner.|
|2H > jH.M.H.M.H > jc jH.H > 5M xx 214M > 623M > j623L|+15 wallbreak|30320|2H AA combo. Really important bnb because it goes coast to coast and ends in a wallbreak.|

### Assist BnBs

|Combo|Advantage|Damage|Notes|
|-----|---------|------|-----|
|5L > 5M > 5H > 5A > 5H xx 214M > 623M > jH > jc jH xx j623L|+15 wallbreak|26465|Basic link attack + assist + extension + ender. Carries corner to corner.|
|5M > 2M xx 214M > 623M > jH.H > 5A > j3U > 5H xx 214M > 623M > 5UU > 5M xx 214L|+15 wallbreak|37805|Your first real corner combo. You'll get the jump for the dark portal usage built into 623M.|
